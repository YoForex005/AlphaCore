## No-loss implication

`LiveIngestHostedService` cannot hide an ingested login from `/traders` merely because a `TraderScore` row has not been written yet: the traders dashboard is keyed off `Mt5Accounts`, and a successful login iteration always upserts a score (including 0-XAU → `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`).

This type does not send, amend, or cancel destination orders. `RealCopyEnabled` is forced false in DI. `TraderStateMachine.CanPromoteToLive` is hardcoded `false`. `PersistDemoShadowAsync` emits `CopyIntent` with `Status = "SHADOW_ONLY"` only when `state == TraderState.SHADOW`; an unscored / `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` login never reaches that branch. Fail-closed ingest (`No dummy data will be substituted`) cannot invent a LIVE book.

No-loss consequence of this angle: **none in the assigned file**. An operator watching `/traders` during or after ingest sees cataloged logins with placeholder scores until `RebuildTraderAsync` lands. Overview XAU/state tiles under-count until scores exist; `totalAccounts` still reflects the catalog. Fail-fast scoring can leave a tail of visible `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` rows — those must not be promoted to LIVE/copy, but they are not omitted from the book. Do not treat this as a capital-loss path.

**Not claimed:** overview state tallies include unscored logins (they do not; that is `GetOverviewAsync`, KPI undercount). One-shot ingest keeps later logins stale until resync/restart (freshness, not hide).
