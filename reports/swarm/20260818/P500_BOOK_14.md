## 4. Deterministic baseline (the only legal ranker)

### 4.1 What it is

`BaselineScorer.Score` is in-process arithmetic over reconstructed **completed XAUUSD** trades.

Risk (cap 100):

```text
+35 martingale (lot > 1.25× after a loss)
+20 averaging-down
+15 lot escalation (lot > 1.5× prior, any PnL)
+10 lot CV > 0.5
+10 SL-use < 0.3
+10 max DD > gross profit (when both > 0)
```

Behavior starts at 100 and subtracts martingale / averaging / lot-CV / SL / loss-size-CV.

Quality:

```text
quality = 50
        + 15 if XAU net PnL > 0
        + 10 if PF >= 1.2
        +  5 if PF >= 1.8
        + 0.20 * behavior
        - 0.25 * risk
        clamp; if N < 3, quality <= 40
```

