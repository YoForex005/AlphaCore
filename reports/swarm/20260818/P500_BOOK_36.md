## 1. What a quote tape is (binding law)

Architecture v2 **§31 Destination Quote Feed** (`MT5_XAUUSD_Trader_Intelligence_cTrader_FIX44_Architecture_v2.md` L1230–1262):

```text
best available destination bid/ask
quote freshness
shadow pricing
slippage reference
pre-trade price checks
```

Must persist: latest quote, receive timestamp, venue timestamp if any, symbol ID, bid, ask. Risk must reject `quote_age > configured_max_quote_age`.

Architecture **§3** target is **future execution-venue-net P&L inside risk limits**, not “who printed dollars on the first 3 challenge trades.” Venue-net is undefined without dest bid/ask.

Official cTrader FIX (`docs/ctrader-fix.md`): QUOTE SSL **5211** is the market-data session (`SecurityList` + `MarketDataRequest`). TRADE SSL **5212** is execution. Logon on 5211 is **session setup**, not a book.

A72 / A23 / A24: three independent entry guards (`QUOTE_STALE`, `SPREAD_TOO_WIDE`, `PRICE_MOVED_TOO_FAR`) plus usability (`bid<=0`, `ask<bid`, unmapped id). Logged-on ≠ fresh. Heartbeat ≠ quote age.

## 4. Why this blocks higher profit

Venue-net (the §3 target) is a function of dest fill quality:

```text
dest_pnl ≈ Σ sign * (exit_touch − entry_touch) * qty  −  spread  −  slippage  −  commission
```

Entry touch is dest **ask** (buy) / dest **bid** (sell). Without those prints:

1. **No shadow expectancy after costs.** `shadowPnl=0` is not “the book is flat.” It is “we never marked.” You cannot rank 70 SHADOW demo names by dest edge.
2. **No cost haircut on scalps.** Synthesis login **322947** avg hold **~163 s**. Gold scalps die in dest spread + 15 s `MaxSourceSignalAge`. You cannot measure that death without a tape.
3. **No honest size.** Current policy is **1:1** (`AllocationFactor = 1m`). Tests prove `Normalize(0.10, 0.05, spec) == 0` (below min at 0.05×) **and** `Normalize(0.05, 1, GoldLots) == 0.05`. The live hop no longer haircuts. 303310’s **2.0** source lots would request **2.0** dest lots (still under `MaxPositionQuantity = 5`). That is a blow-up ticket on gold, sized **without** dest mid, tick value, or margin.
4. **Scorer is not a dest-profit gate.** Quality can be 95.50 with **negative** dashboard `netSourcePnl` (all-symbol sum vs XAU-only score). SHADOW on `demo\yo-2step` is challenge PnL, not Pepperstone PnL.
5. **LoggedOn does not monetize.** `QuoteHealthy=true` + 8463 accounts is a census, not a book.

**Higher profit is therefore: do not send, build a standing tape, then shadow a tiny filtered subset after dest costs.** It is not “copy more logins because FIX is up.” It is not “copy 1:1 because the policy says one-to-one.”

## 6. Sizing without a tape (why 1:1 lots is not a risk size)

`XauUsdOneToOneCopyPolicy`:

| Constant | Value (this tree, slot 36 re-read) |
|---|---|
| `AllocationFactor` | **1m** (not 0.05) |
| `GoldOuncesPerLot` | **100** |
| `GoldLots` | min **0.01**, max **5**, step **0.01**, precision **2** |
| `MinCompletedXauTrades` | **20** |

`QuantityNormalizer` has no quote parameters. Adjacent `VolumeConverter` converts MT5 native / 10_000 → lots. Neither computes:

- dest notional = price × oz × lots (needs dest mid),
- dest spread cost = `(ask − bid) × oz × lots` (needs dest book),
- margin at dest (needs dest price + leverage),
- whether 303310’s **2.0** source lots × **1.0** = **2.00** dest is acceptable on a 2-dollar-wide news book.

So “cannot size” is literal: the only dest qty function is a **dimensionless** lot copy. It cannot refuse a wide book or a gapped book. Slot 16’s 0.05 haircut is **gone**. Wanting that haircut back does not create an edge either — it still needs a dest tape to know what 0.05 lots costs.
