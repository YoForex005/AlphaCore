The documented gates are **account-level circuit breakers** that fire **after** dest equity has already moved:

| Documented limit | Default | When it fires | Quality of the *source* name |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Max Daily Loss | 5% of day-start equity | After dest DD exists | Not consulted |
| Max Total Loss | 10% of initial balance | After dest DD exists | Not consulted |
