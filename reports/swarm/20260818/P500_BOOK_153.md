## 4. Why MAE-based stops are **not** the loss-reduction tool

### 4.1 What an MAE stop would require (and we do not have)

An honest dest stop from source MAE would need, **per copied lifecycle**:

1. Same-broker bid/ask prints over `[opened_at, closed_at]` (A45 §4.1).
2. Durable `mt5_xau_ticks` rows keyed `(broker_id, source_symbol, time_msc)`.
3. `MfeMaeCalculator` that **refuses** deals-only and **refuses** dest-quote gap-fill.
4. `feature_quality == Exact` and both numbers non-null.
5. A dest policy that maps source MAE to a **Pepperstone** stop — different book, different spread, later fill. Even Exact source MAE is **not** a dest stop without a separate, labeled dest excursion.

None of 1–4 exist. Step 5 is not designed. Claiming “we will cut losers at 1.1× MAE” is **greenwash**.

`RiskEngine` (`D:\Prop\src\Domain\Risk\RiskEngine.cs`) has **zero** MFE/MAE reads. Its stops are loss caps (`MaxLossPerTrader=500`, `MaxDailyExecutionLoss=2000`), quote age, spread, size, and martingale **flags**. Caps fire after dest is already bleeding. They are not MAE. `MaxSlippage = 1.5` is declared and **unread**.

Copy policy `StopLoss` is a pass-through of `signal.StopLoss` (`XauUsdOneToOneCopyPolicy.Evaluate` L160). The hopper fills that field from `trade.FinalSl ?? trade.InitialSl` (`CopyTradingService.cs` L234). That is the source ticket’s SL field, **not** a computed MAE multiple.
