## 5. Why this is adverse selection (mechanism)

### 5.1 Selection, not skill

A prop-challenge account is **option-like**:

1. Trader pays (or is granted) a demo / 2-step / pass-first seat.
2. Objective is **hit a profit target** under a max-DD rule — not maximize long-run expectancy after venue costs.
3. Paths that print early (grid, martingale, lot explosion, mixed-symbol gambling) are **over-represented** among accounts that still look green at snapshot time.
4. Paths that then size-up after a loss are exactly `RISK_BLOCKED` (`TraderStateMachine.FromBaseline`: `risk ≥ 80` **or** `(Martingale ∧ MaxDrawdown > 0 ∧ NetPnl < 0)`).
5. Copying the **currently green** challenge names copies the **conditional** winners. The dest does **not** receive the challenge firm’s optionality (they can fail the trader). The dest receives the **next** ticket at live costs.

That is the definition of adverse selection: you observe the pass, you inherit the blow.
