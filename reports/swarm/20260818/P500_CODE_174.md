## Lower-loss implication

**Capital is not at risk from dest fills today.** That is the only good news, and it is **absence**, not a proven dest book.

- `destinationRealPnl` **0** + `REAL_COPY` **false** + copy-hop `NewOrderSingleImplemented=false` + persist `AllowFixSend=false` ⇒ Pepperstone cannot receive a copy `35=D` from this pipeline. This slot did not send and did not build NewOrderSingle.

- Loss-reduction from shadow is **unavailable**: you cannot reject a future live copy on dest drawdown, dest slippage, or dest MTM because those series do not exist. The §23 gate “minimum shadow net P&L / maximum shadow DD” cannot fire on real venue data.
- Decision risk (not capital risk): an operator who reads “Dest. real P&L 0.00” + “QUOTE healthy” + 8463 accounts may believe the dest book is live-flat and shadow is venue-priced. Both beliefs are **false**. That is how a later `REAL_COPY=true` plus a new sender would lose money **without** a dest-quote sample.

Keep `REAL_COPY_EXECUTION_ENABLED=false`. Do not add NewOrderSingle until Phase 4 persists a live XAU book, Phase 5 marks shadow on that book (entry **and** exit), and `DestinationRealPnl` is a dest-fill rollup (honest 0 only when dest positions are actually empty).
