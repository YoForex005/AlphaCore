### 4.2 Score is not dest PnL and not a size

```text
quality = 50
        + 15 if XAU net > 0
        + 10 if PF >= 1.2
        +  5 if PF >= 1.8
        + 0.20 * behavior
        - 0.25 * risk

```

Dashboard `netSourcePnl` sums **all symbols**. Score uses **completed XAU only**. That is why Achiever **302252** is `SHADOW` **95.50** with dashboard PnL **−68.46**. Sizing from `earlyScore` sizes from a heuristic that **already prints SHADOW on a loser**.

`CanPromoteToLive` is hard-`false`. High Achiever score → `SHADOW`, never a legal live ticket.
