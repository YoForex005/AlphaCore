## Verdict

`GetOverviewAsync` paints `DestinationRealPnl`, `XauGross`, and `XauNet` as **literal `0`**. Per-trader `ShadowPnl` is **literal `0`**. Risk `DailyPnl` / drawdown / XAU exposure are **literal `0`**. FIX `ExecutionEnabled` is **literal `false`**. `RealCopyEnabled` is forwarded from `LiveRuntimeStatus` (forced **false** at DI and again after FIX logon). The only money the leaderboard *does* compute is **source** `Sum(NetRealizedPnl)` on completed reconstructed trades — challenge / MT5 paper, **not** dest-venue net.

A +$100 demo challenge print therefore cannot be dest edge in this read model:

1. Dest book is not queried. There is no dest-fill table read. The overview field that would show real venue PnL is hardcoded **0**, matching live `destinationRealPnl 0`.
2. Source `NetRealizedPnl` is `Gross + Commission + Swap + Fees` with reconstructor **`Fees = 0m` always**. Even the source “net” can miss venue fees.
3. Leaderboard PnL is **not** XAU-filtered and is **not** reduced by dest bid/ask, dest commission, or `SourceVsShadowSlippage`.
4. Overview `ShadowPnl` is `SUM(shadow_orders.SourceVsShadowSlippage)`, a **slippage tape**, not dest realized PnL. Demo / live dest fills remain 0.
5. Seed dest quote used by `GetFixSessionsAsync` is Bid **2399.45** / Ask **2399.85** (spread **$0.40/oz**). Crossing that spread on the 0.10-lot fixture is **~$4** round-trip before dest commission; three A22 trades ≈ **~$12** just to pay the painted book. `ShadowCopyEngine.DefaultLatencySlippagePoints = 0.05` adds more on delayed entries. **+$100 − dest spread − dest commission − slippage is not a measured dest fill.**
6. A22 / `TraderStateMachine` ceiling for the +$100 clean book is **SHADOW**. `CanPromoteToLive => false`. Caller: SHADOW all demo; `REAL_COPY false`; FIX LoggedOn ≠ send.

Live census (given): 8463 accounts, Achiever scoring, Starwave **deals-done scored 0**. This class will happily paint thousands of `NetSourcePnl` cells from whatever reconstructed rows exist, and Starwave logins with no deals as `pnl=0` / `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`. None of that is dest edge.

## Profit implication

**None. +$100 on the challenge / reconstruction fixture is not Pepperstone profit.**

Destination realized profit requires a dest fill (TRADE `35=D` accepted, booked, closed) minus dest spread, dest commission, and slippage versus the source print. This class:

- never sends;
- never reads a dest ledger;
- reports `DestinationRealPnl = 0`, `XauGross = 0`, `XauNet = 0`;
- reports per-trader `ShadowPnl = 0`;
- reports risk `DailyPnl = 0`.

If an operator copied the A22 +$100 book at the seed quote, they would **pay** ~$0.40/oz × 10 oz ≈ **$4** per 0.10-lot round-trip just to cross Bid/Ask, plus `0.05` modeled latency, plus dest commission this reconstructor stores as **0**. Three first-3 trades consume a double-digit slice of that $100 **before** any dest edge exists. Live `destinationRealPnl` remaining **0** is the honest P&amp;L of this process.

Do **not** size live XAU from `TraderRowDto.NetSourcePnl` or from first-three `NetRealizedPnl` highlights. Those columns are source reconstructed paper. Starwave scored-0 rows are zeros, not a hidden winner.
