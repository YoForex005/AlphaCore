## 3. Mechanism: pass the target, then blow

This is not a vibe. It is the **incentive** of a 2-step / pass-first demo plus the **measured** left tail.

### 3.1 Why challenge tape is adverse for a live dest

A challenge login is paid (in “pass / funded / payout”) for:

1. Hitting a **profit target** on a **demo** book (often subsidized commission / different markups).
2. Staying inside a **daily / total DD** rail until that target prints.

That objective is **not** “maximize dest-net after Pepperstone spread, delay, and a 0.05-lot cap.”

Optimal challenge behavior that **fails** as a copy source:

| Challenge-optimal | What dest copy inherits |
|---|---|
| High win-rate grids / same-second multi-ticket | Looks like SHADOW; scorer misses parallel-ticket averaging (`WasAveragedDown` is same-position only) |
| Size-up after a loss to recover the hurdle | `Martingale=true` → `RISK_BLOCKED` **after** the book is already −EV |
| Gold scalps (median hold ~minutes) | Die in dest spread + 15 s `MaxSourceSignalAge` |
| First-3 luck / +$100 residual | `quality += 15` if source XAU net > 0; leaderboard sorts `EarlyScore` desc |
| Stop trading or blow after pass | Dest would still be **on** for the next size-up if you copied “winners” |

The **survivors you want to copy** (printed a target, still look clean) are **selected** for the pass. The **same population** after they press is the **−$241k** blocked bucket.

That is the textbook definition of **adverse selection**: the observable “winner” attribute is the one the population was built to fake.
