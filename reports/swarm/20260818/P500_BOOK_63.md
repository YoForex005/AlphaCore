## 3. Scoring: why SHADOW 93.50 is legal

Martingale / lot-escalation (`BaselineScorer` L86–94) walk **completed** trades ordered by `ClosedAt`:

```text
martingale     := prior.Net < 0  AND  next.MaxLots > prior.MaxLots × 1.25
lotEscalation  := next.MaxLots > prior.MaxLots × 1.5
averagingDown  := Any(WasAveragedDown)
```

A22 specified `MARTINGALE_VOLUME_RATIO = 1.80` and `FLAG_AVERAGING_DOWN` floor **75**. Product is **1.25** additive **+35** (martingale) / **+20** (averaging), not `Max(risk, 75)`. Neither spec nor product catches a flat 0.05 hedge spray.

```text

        + 15 if NetPnl > 0
        + 10 if PF >= 1.2
        +  5 if PF >= 1.8
        + 0.20 × behavior
        − 0.25 × risk
SHADOW if eligible AND quality >= 70 AND risk < 40
```

If flags stay false and SL is unused: `risk += 10` (SL < 0.3) → risk **10**; `behavior` ≈ 90. Then `50+15+10+5+18−2.5 = 95.50` — the familiar 95.50 identity. The 93.50 is the **expanding 102-trade book**, not skill at trade #3 (`P500_S020`).
