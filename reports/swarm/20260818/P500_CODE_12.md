## Binding context (why 95.5 ≠ dest PnL)

Architecture v2 §15 / §23 and A22 I2/I5/I6/I8/I9 (same law the 11-line doc compresses):

- Trade #3 emits `EARLY_SCORE_ELIGIBLE`, **not** `PROVEN_PROFITABLE`.
- Trade #3 + high score → **SHADOW only**. Never automatic real capital.
- As-of `T` may use only information available at `T`. **No trade #n+1, no future balance, no future drawdown.**
- The three scores are **source-behavior**. Shadow/live destination P&L is **never inside the three formulas**.
- `early_quality_score` must **not** rank traders by raw net P&L. The *product target* is future copyable profitability; that is a **later label**, not the first-3 measurement.

Architecture §19 (ML objective, Phase 6 — not first-3):


That split is the answer. First-3 features are **inputs**. Future dest net on trades #4–#23 is the **unmeasured target**. A 95.5 on demo `yo-2step` is the input side only.
