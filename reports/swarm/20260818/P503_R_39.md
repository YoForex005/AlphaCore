## 0. Verdict (ADMIT / REMOVE / FLATTEN implication)

**Binding law**

```text
ADMIT   = AllowNewOpens + Open/Increase family only while roster Status=ADMITTED
          and the trader is still eligible. New dest risk is membership, not a close.
REMOVE  = stop ADMIT. AllowNewOpens=false. Status=REMOVED:<reason>.
          Must NOT be implemented as “reject every CopyIntentAction”.
FLATTEN = dest-only CloseExposure of already-copied dest/shadow inventory.
          MUST remain legal after REMOVE. Qty = dest remaining (A71), not source lots.
          Never DealerSend / never flatten the MT5 source book.
```
