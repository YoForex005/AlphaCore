## Verdict

**FAIL — a +$100 demo-challenge `NetSourcePnl` tile is source-tape dollars, not venue-net edge.**

`EfDashboardQueries` is the only dashboard projection of trader and book PnL. It:

1. Surfaces **source** `SUM(reconstructed_trades.NetRealizedPnl)` as the traders-page **Net P&L**.
2. Hard-wires overview **`DestinationRealPnl = 0`**, **`XauGross = 0`**, **`XauNet = 0`**.
3. Labels overview “Shadow P&L” as `SUM(shadow_orders.SourceVsShadowSlippage)` — a **touch-vs-source slippage sum**, not dest realized after spread/commission/swap.
4. Hard-wires per-trader `ShadowPnl = 0` and risk `DailyPnl` / dest XAU exposure = **0**.
5. Never subtracts cTrader venue spread, commission, swap, latency, or slippage from the +$100 challenge print.

A prop-challenge / `demo\yo-2step` account that prints **+$100** on Achiever MT5 will therefore rank and display as a **+$100 winner**. After Pepperstone / cTrader costs that number is **not** copyable edge. Caller-measured `destinationRealPnl = 0` is the **constructor literal**, not a measured flat dest book.

FIX **LoggedOn** + 8463 Achiever-catalog accounts + `REAL_COPY false` do not convert challenge dollars into venue-net dollars.

### 3. Source “net” itself is not venue-net

## Profit implication

**No destination-venue profit is measured or implied by a +$100 challenge print.**

- Overview `DestinationRealPnl` / `XauGross` / `XauNet` are uncomputed zeros. There is no dest book to harvest.
- A +$100 on Achiever `demo\yo-2step` / `contest\yo-2step` is **challenge-tape** (often a phase-1 profit target order of magnitude). Gold 0.10 lot ≈ $1/point: **+$100 is ~10 points of XAU**. Typical dest half-spread + commission + 80 ms shadow delay + adverse slip on a scalp **can erase that** on the **first** copied round-trip. This query never computes that haircut.
- Ranking 8463 accounts by `EarlyScore` (boosted when source net > 0) selects **challenge winners**, not venue-net survivors. Copying the +$100 name later does **not** lock in +$100 at Pepperstone.
- Starwave deals-done scored **0** contributes **no** source tape and **no** dest tape. It does not create a second book that could validate the Achiever +$100.
- SHADOW-all-demo slippage Σ is **not** “we already subtracted venue costs from the +$100.” It is source-vs-touch on a seed/stale quote. Mark-to-market dest PnL is unused here.
- Enabling `REAL_COPY` to “realize” the +$100 would send size against a book this dashboard has **never** netted. That is the opposite of proven edge.

Profit capture from slot 68: **none**. Treating +$100 as edge is a **selection error**, not a profit engine.
