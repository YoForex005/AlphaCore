## 4. Why “they look profitable” is the trap (mechanism)

A prop-challenge account is **option-like**:

1. Trader pays (or is granted) a demo / 2-step / pass-first seat.
2. Objective is **hit a profit target** under a max-DD rule — not maximize long-run expectancy after venue costs.
3. Paths that print early (grid, martingale, lot explosion, mixed-symbol gambling) are **over-represented** among accounts that still look green at snapshot time.
4. Paths that then size-up after a loss are exactly `RISK_BLOCKED`.
5. The dest does **not** receive the challenge firm’s optionality (they can fail the trader). The dest receives the **next** ticket at live costs.

That is adverse selection: you observe the pass, you inherit the blow.

Copying 303310 because it is +$41,634 on 22 XAU is the worst form of the error: the size that made the challenge number is the size that blows a 1:1 Pepperstone book. Copying 322947 because it is +$4,950 on 194 tickets copies **~163 s** gold scalps into a 15 s stale-signal + spread cost. Copying 303274 because it is +$1,228 copies an unnamed same-second 0.05 grid.

`RISK_BLOCKED` already outweighs the SHADOW head: **−$241,580 vs +$78,276**. Copy-all 8463 imports that tail. ADMIT of “just the green demo names” still imports the **conditional** winners of a target-pass process.
