## 3. Why a single dest-loser flatten is the wrong roster verb

### 3.1 It is a dest MAE stop. MAE is Unavailable.

Architecture v2 §1.5: do not calculate MFE/MAE from closed deals; do not fabricate if the open tape is missing. A45 / P500_BOOK_73: product writes `FeatureQuality.Unavailable`; dest SL is `FinalSl ?? InitialSl` (source fill clone). Flattening dest when dest (or source) mark ≤ −$150 **is** an MAE-based dest stop. That claim is **FORBIDDEN**.

### 3.2 It desyncs 1:1

Policy law (`XauUsdOneToOneCopyPolicy` L57–61): copy **next** XAU events 1:1; close when the **source** closes; do not pick winners (lookahead). Dest-flat-while-source-open is the inverse lookahead: dest crystallizes a loser the source may still hold to target.

After dest flatten:

| Next source event | Hop today |
|---|---|
| Source still open, `:open` exists | No re-open (idempotency). Dest stays flat. |
| Source closes winner | Close loop writes `:close` anyway (if trader still ADMITTED). Orphan dest close / no dest 721. |
| Source scales in | New `:open` on a **different** `PositionId` only. Same ticket does not re-enter. |

Dest missed the recovery **and** any further loss. That is insurance, not the 1:1 book. Do not advertise it as copy-edge.

### 3.3 $150 at HEAD `AllocationFactor=1` is noise

Gold PnL ≈ `Δ$ / oz × lots × 100`. Cap trip = `$150 / (lots × 100)`:

| Dest lots (α=1) | Move that trips −$150 |
|---|---|
| 0.01 | **$150.00 / oz** (almost never) |
| 0.05 | **$30.00 / oz** |
| 0.10 | **$15.00 / oz** (common) |
| 1.00 | **$1.50 / oz** (minutes) |
| 5.00 (instrument max) | **$0.30 / oz** (spread + one tick) |

HEAD `XauUsdOneToOneCopyPolicy.AllocationFactor = 1m`. A 1-lot or 5-lot dest copy would flatten on noise. That is **DEST_NOISE_FLATTEN**, not ruin protection. Ruin protection at α=1 is **tiny dest size** (0.01–0.05 of source) plus dest SL at send, not a $150 USD tripwire.

### 3.4 Architecture did not authorize auto-flatten-from-threshold

A48 §2: *“Engine daily-loss auto-flatten — `GLOBAL_STOP` engages **stop-new only**. Auto-flatten from a threshold is a trading-policy decision the architecture did not authorize.”*

A23 §8.2: `EMERGENCY_FLATTEN` is SuperAdmin + confirm, dest remaining, known dest ids. Not a 20 s poll comparing one decimal to −150.

A71: CLOSE family needs **linked dest remaining**. `FLATTEN_LOSS_CUT` sized from source `RequestedQuantity` is the **wrong qty**.

`RiskEngine.MaxLossPerTrader=500` is **realized**, after-the-fact, and `Reject()` **without** `IsIncreasing` — if `Evaluate` were ever called on the flatten close, a trader already ≤ −$500 would **freeze the exit** (A71 G21–G22 FAIL). Slot 12 already removed “per-tick dest stop” as a claim. Do not pile a second unauthorized flatten on top.

### 3.5 Stale / unpriced mark must not flatten

A24: unpriced unrealized is **null**, not 0. Stale quote still publishes a number with quality `STALE_QUOTE`. `ShouldFlattenOpenCopy` has no quality bit. A missing dest quote today would either never fire or fire on a seeder 2399 mid — both wrong.
