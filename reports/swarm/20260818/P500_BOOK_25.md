## 1. Official cTrader FIX (Spotware RoE + Pepperstone form)

Sources (fetched this slot):

- https://help.ctrader.com/fix/ — FIX 4.4; lists **trade copiers** as a use-case and then says **other Spotware APIs may fit copy better**. That is not a license to fire market orders from a one-shot Logon.
- https://help.ctrader.com/fix/specification/ — Rules of Engagement: two session qualifiers, Logon `35=A`, NewOrderSingle `35=D`, Execution Report `35=8`.
- https://help.ctrader.com/fix/sending-and-receiving-messages/ — two TCP clients / two ports; TargetCompID “usually `cServer`”; sample header `56=CSERVER`; qualifier `QUOTE` / `TRADE`.
- Community confirmation of the **SSL port pair** (not a fill): QUOTE **5211**, TRADE **5212**, TargetCompID **`cServer`** (`https://community.ctrader.com/forum/fix-api/46810/`).
- Broker-issued Pepperstone form, pinned in architecture §25 and `docs/ctrader-fix.md`.

### 1.1 Two sessions, two TLS ports

| Session | Official role | TLS (production default) | Plain (not production default) | Allowed after Logon (RoE) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **QUOTE** | Market data + SecurityList | **5211** | 5201 | `35=x` SecurityListRequest, `35=V` MarketDataRequest, inbound `35=W`/`35=X` |
| **TRADE** | Orders / status / positions | **5212** | 5202 | `35=D` NewOrderSingle, `35=H`/`35=AF` status, inbound `35=8` ExecutionReport |

They are **independent FIX sessions**: separate sockets, sequence numbers, heartbeats, and ownership. Sharing one seq store between QUOTE and TRADE is a spec fail (architecture §27). Two simultaneous TRADE owners duplicate reports / orders (architecture §27 / A25).

### 1.3 Official message map: Logon ≠ fill

RoE (`https://help.ctrader.com/fix/specification/`):

| MsgType | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `35=A` | Logon | Begin a session. Heartbeat `108` default 30s. `141=Y` resets seq. `553` = **numeric account id**, `49` = `env.brokerUid.login`. |
| `35=5` | Logout | Invalid Logon → Logout with `58=Text`. Not a fill. |
| `35=0` / `35=1` | Heartbeat / TestRequest | Liveness. Not a fill. |
| `35=V` / `35=W` / `35=X` | Market data | QUOTE tape. Not a fill. |
| `35=x` / `35=y` | SecurityList | Instrument **numeric** id in tag 55. Not `XAUUSD` text. Not a fill. |
| **`35=D`** | **NewOrderSingle** | **TRADE only.** This is the order. Product copy hop does **not** send it. |
| **`35=8`** | **Execution Report** | **This is the fill** when `150=F` and `39=2`. Official market-order example: request `35=D` then two `35=8` (`150=0` New, then `150=F` Trade). |

Official Logon prose: *“Once the logon is complete, quote and trade flows can proceed for the lifecycle of the session.”* **Can proceed** ≠ **have filled**. A Logon reply `35=A` has no `ClOrdID`, no `LastPx`, no `CumQty`.
