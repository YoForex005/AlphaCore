## No-loss implication

This contract cannot silently drop open risk on accounts 201+: it never enumerates “first 200 accounts” and never trims `IReadOnlyList<Mt5PositionDto>`. A 200-account position window at this layer would leave `mt5_positions_current` stale or empty for the tail, so recon / exposure / copy could act while blind to live inventory — a capital-loss path. Absence of that cap here is the correct no-loss surface: callers **must** be able to snapshot every login (per-login or `groupMask`). If a future caller reintroduces `accounts.Take(200)`, that is a caller defect, not a contract-encoded limit. These interfaces cannot open or flatten live positions.
