## 4. Why naked `$150` is UNSAFE_IF_WIRED on this book

Contract: `GoldOuncesPerLot = 100`. `AllocationFactor = 1m` (1:1 lots). `GoldLots` / `MaxPositionQuantity` max **5.00**. Honest dest USD (A24 conservative mark):

```text
long  UPL$ = (bid − entry) × destLots × 100 − costs
short UPL$ = (entry − ask) × destLots × 100 − costs
```

`$1/oz` on dest lots = **$100 × destLots**. Cap math if `ShouldFlattenOpenCopy` is fed **honest dest $**:

| Dest lots | $ / $1 oz | $150 = gold move | Seed spread 0.40 (`DemoSeeder` 2399.45/2399.85) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.01 | $1 | $150 / oz | $0.40 dest |
| 0.05 | $5 | $30 / oz | $2 dest |
| 1.00 | $100 | **$1.50 / oz** | $40 dest |
| 5.00 | $500 | **$0.30 / oz** | **$200 dest** (already > $150 on the open print) |

5-lot dest would flatten **immediately** on the seeder spread. 1-lot dest dies on a $1.50 noise print. That is dest-churn, not risk control. It systematically cuts dest while the source still holds (1:1 desync): dest misses source recovery and still copies the next ticket.

`ShadowCopyEngine.MarkToMarket` is `(px − entry) * sign * qty` with **no ounces**. If `qty` is lots, UPL is **100× understated**. Wiring `ShouldFlattenOpenCopy(MarkToMarket(lots))` would need a **$3,000 / oz** move on 0.05 lots to trip $150 — the helper would be dead. Do not wire it that way. A24: unpriced unrealized is **`null`**, not 0. The helper has no quality bit; a missing quote must **not** flatten.

`RiskEngine.MaxLossPerTrader = 500` is a **different** dest-dollar number. It `PauseTrader`s **every** action including close (L117–118) if ever fed honest dest loss. Hop today passes `TraderRealizedLoss = Min(0, this ticket NetRealizedPnl)` and zeros dest book fields. Do not reuse L117–124 as this ticket stop.
