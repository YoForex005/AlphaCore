## Profit implication

Hard-false **blocks live profit**. It does **not** protect profit.

- 8463 catalogued accounts and Achiever scoring cannot produce destination realized P&L while `FromBaseline` maxes at `SHADOW` and `CanPromoteToLive` is a constant `false`.
- `destinationRealPnl` is compiled as `0`. There is no dest fill ledger, no dest close, no dest mark-to-market persist (`ShadowCopyEngine.SimulateEntry` only; `ShadowPosition` is never constructed).
- SHADOW copy intents are `SHADOW_ONLY`. They are not ExecutionIntent and they are not `35=D`.
- Starwave scored 0 means even the **source** book is not ranked for that broker; there is nothing to promote even if the pin were later flipped.
- Opportunity cost is the entire Achiever XAU winner set that would have been copied had R6 + RBAC + risk + `REAL_COPY` ever been implemented. That cost is **unmeasured** because dest P&L is a literal.

Do **not** claim “we are protecting edge by staying in SHADOW.” SHADOW here is simulated entry slippage vs source VWAP, not a proven dest-edge book.

## Lower-loss implication

Hard-false **protects capital** (lower-loss) by making auto-LIVE unreachable.

- Three disciplined winners go to `SHADOW`, not `LIVE` (unit lock). High early score is pinned `AfterHighEarlyScore() => SHADOW`.
- Losing martingale (0.10 / 0.20 / 0.40) is `RISK_BLOCKED` and never reaches shadow persist.
- FIX may be **LoggedOn**; that is a session flag, not a send license. `RealCopyEnabled` is forced false at DI and again after logon.
- Live capital at risk from this process: **NONE** (`SAFE_BY_ABSENCE` of `NewOrderSingle` / `35=D`, plus the unused pin, plus SHADOW_ONLY persist).
- Residual: the pin is **vacuous**. Application never calls it. Persist would honor a future `SuggestedState = LIVE` without consulting `CanPromoteToLive`. The real floor today is “we forgot live” + send-path absence, **not** an audited A22 R5-before-R6 + R6-approve machine.
