## 1. Why this slot is lower-loss, not higher-profit

Architecture §3 target is **future destination-net PnL inside risk limits**, not first-3 dollars:

> Given the behavior visible up to now, which traders have the highest probability of generating **positive future execution-venue-net P&L** while remaining inside risk limits?

Two different questions:

| Question | Mechanism | Moves dest EV? |
|---|---|---|
| **Higher profit** | Copy a tiny, filtered subset with +EV **after** dest costs | Yes, **iff** that subset exists and is proven on a real quote tape |
| **Lower loss** (this slot) | At most one dest order per intent; never a second `35=D` while venue outcome is unknown | Caps **duplicate** loss. Does **not** flip the sign of a −EV book |

Blind retry after “I sent, then TCP died” is how a 1.00-lot 1:1 hedge becomes **2.00** (or a 5-lot `GoldLots` cap becomes **10**) on **one** retail Pepperstone login. Official cTrader Logon is `141=Y` (seq reset) — `CTraderFixSession.BuildLogon` already sends `(141, "Y")` at L105. FIX resend **cannot** recover the original `35=D`. Persist-before-send + `OrderStatusRequest` (`35=H`) is the only recovery. That is **loss containment**.

It is not a filter. It does not see `RISK_BLOCKED`. It does not see demo `yo-2step`. It does not haircut spread. Copying all **8463** logins with perfect ClOrdID hygiene still imports the **−$241,580** martingale tail **once**. Once is enough to ruin the dest.

Wanting both “no duplicate fills” and “the book is profitable” does not make the second true.
