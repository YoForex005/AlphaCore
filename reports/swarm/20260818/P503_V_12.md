So if a roster loser is removed while the **source ticket is still open**:

- roster row = `REMOVED:…` (new dest opens stop — good)
- dest fill in `DemoCopyLedger` stays `DestClosed=false`

That is leftover dest risk, not leftover roster membership. Membership hole is §2.2–2.4; this is the flatten hole.
