## No-loss implication

This store cannot hide an unscored login from the desk in a way that then silently copies that login: it has no dashboard projection. Missing `TraderScore` rows also cannot open live dest risk here. `PersistDemoShadowAsync` writes a `ScoreUpdate` outbox for any state, then **returns before** `CopyIntent` / `ShadowOrder` unless `state == TraderState.SHADOW`. Intents that do get written are `Status = "SHADOW_ONLY"` with a 15 s expiry and a paper `ShadowCopyEngine.SimulateEntry` fill — **no FIX send, no LIVE, no `ExecutionIntent`**. An account that exists without a score is persisted and listed (`ListLoginsAsync`); it is not copied. Worst case from this angle in this file is an operator seeing `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` (dashboard left-join) or a zeroed overview state bucket — **visibility / census, not a live flatten or size-up**.
