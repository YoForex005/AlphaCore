## 0. Verdict

**ADMIT implication: `OpenExposure` / `IncreaseExposure` only while the trader is roster-admitted (or Keep) and still eligible. REMOVE implication: stop new opens (`AllowNewOpens=false`); do not cancel, freeze, or abandon the dest book. FLATTEN implication: after REMOVE, dest remaining must still be reducible via `CopyIntentAction.CloseExposure` (destination-only; never flatten the MT5 source). HEAD inverts the law: the same ineligibility that emits `RosterAction.RemoveAndFlatten` also rejects `CloseExposure` in policy and skips the hopper, so dest cannot flatten.**

| Lever | Law | HEAD product |
|---|---|---|
| **ADMIT** / Keep | New dest risk allowed (`AllowNewOpens=true`, `FlattenDestination=false`) | Policy + hopper will construct `OpenExposure` **only** while `IsTraderEligible` is true. Roster engine is **DEAD** (not wired). |
| **REMOVE** | Stop **new** copy. Trader leaves the admit set. Dest book stays until flatten. | `RemoveAndFlatten` exists as a **label**. Hopper `{SHADOW, LIVE_CANDIDATE, LIVE}` + `IsTraderEligible` `continue` drop the trader **including** the close loop. |
| **FLATTEN** | Dest-only `CloseExposure` of **known** dest remaining. Must remain legal **after** REMOVE / `PAUSED` / `RISK_BLOCKED` / `DISQUALIFIED` / book-turned-negative. Never source MT5. | **MISSING as a run.** `FlattenDestination=true` has no executor. Policy `Evaluate` requires eligibility for **all** actions, including `CloseExposure`. Risk loss/DD predicates can still `Reject` a close. Send bit requires `KillSwitch==None`. |

One line: **REMOVE is not a license to trap dest risk. FLATTEN is dest-only `CloseExposure` after REMOVE. ADMIT must not be the only state in which close is constructible.**

Today dest realized PnL is **$0** by `SAFE_BY_ABSENCE` (no product `NewOrderSingle` / no hop `35=D`). The inversion is still a **live-account landmine** if a sender is later bolted on without splitting OPEN vs CLOSE eligibility.

Do not “fix” this by making every `CloseExposure` sendable, or by flattening MT5 source logins. Identity (known dest id, dest remaining qty, no double-close) still binds.

## 1. Binding law (ADMIT / REMOVE / FLATTEN)

Architecture §64 / §72.18: reduce/close is a different family from open/increase. Residual dest risk after the source (or the trader) is gone is a **defect**.

A71 G19–G20 (normative):

| # | Check | OPEN | INCREASE | REDUCE | CLOSE | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G19 | Trader not `LIVE` (live path) | B | B | A | A | Residual dest risk must still be reducible. |
| G20 | `PAUSED` / `RISK_BLOCKED` / `DISQUALIFIED` | B | B | A | A | Pause stops **new** copy, not dest exits. |

A71 G21–G23: `MAX_LOSS_PER_TRADER` / `MAX_DAILY_EXECUTION_LOSS` / `MAX_PORTFOLIO_DRAWDOWN` block OPEN family and engage stop-new; they **Approve** CLOSE. “Do **not** trap a losing dest position.”

A48 §3.1: `STOP_NEW_EXECUTION` allows `REDUCE_EXPOSURE` / `CLOSE_EXPOSURE` of already mapped dest positions. Flatten is not “new copy.”

A48 / dest-only flatten law: kill switches and roster flatten apply to the **destination** execution account. **Never flatten source MT5.** Shadow is not live.
