    {

            return TraderState.RISK_BLOCKED;

`docs/scoring.md`: “Martingale / large sequential size-up after losses ⇒ `RISK_BLOCKED`.” Correct as a **loss-shape detector**. It is **not** a dest-profit model. It fires **after** the book has already gone negative-net with a martingale flag.
