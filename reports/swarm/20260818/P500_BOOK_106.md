## 1. What “adverse selection” means on this book

Adverse selection here is a **measured population-plus-incentive** fact, not a vibe:

1. **The catalog is a challenge factory, not a live gold desk.** Achiever’s manager ACL is **zero** funded `real\` groups. The mass product is `demo\yo-2step` (**6295 / 6512 = 96.67%**). Those logins are issued to **hit a profit target** on a virtual book. After a drawdown the rational challenge action is to **recover the target** (size-up after losses).
2. **The scorer rewards the look of a pass.** `BaselineScorer` never sees `GroupName`, challenge phase, or dest spread. Three completed XAU tickets can print `SHADOW` at **95.50** while the challenge account is **underwater from a $1,000 start**.
3. **The left tail is the same product, later.** `TraderStateMachine.FromBaseline` sends `risk ≥ 80` **or** `(Martingale ∧ MaxDrawdown > 0 ∧ NetPnl < 0)` to `RISK_BLOCKED`. Mid-scoring pin: **29** such names, **all** `martingale=true`, source sum **−$241,580**.

Copying the **8463** catalog (or even every scored XAU name) copies the **blocked tail**. That is how dest capital dies.

Architecture §20 **forbids** using final challenge result / eventual pass-fail as a feature (correct: no leakage). That does **not** make demo SHADOW a dest edge. It means “they passed / they look like they will pass” is **not** a legal `y` and **not** a copy signal.
