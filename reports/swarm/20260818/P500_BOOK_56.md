## 4. Cannot size without a tape

Venue-net (the §3 target) is a function of dest fill quality:

```text
dest_pnl ≈ Σ sign * (exit_touch − entry_touch) * qty  −  spread  −  slippage  −  commission
notional   = dest_mid × oz_per_lot × lots
spread_$   = (ask − bid) × oz_per_lot × lots
```

Entry touch is dest **ask** (buy) / dest **bid** (sell). All three formulas need dest bid/ask. They are **undefined** when both are null.
