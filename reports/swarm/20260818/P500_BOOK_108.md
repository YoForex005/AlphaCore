## 4. Why a $2,000 / $500 latch is not an edge (math)

### 4.1 Edge vs stop

An **edge** is positive dest expectancy **after** venue costs, **before** the first lot: filter + size + proven shadow tape.

A **loss cap** is: “keep running the same process until the account is down $X, then stop new.” Expected dest PnL of a −EV copier with a stop at −$2,000 is **about −$2,000**, not +EV. The stop changes the **left tail of the dest equity path**. It does not change the **sign**.

```text
E[dest | copy-all, daily latch $2000]  ≈  −min($2000, |copy-all EV|)
                                        <  0
E[dest | copy RISK_BLOCKED]            ≈  −$241,580 × dest_scale  (uncapped)
                                        or −$2000/day until the latch
Neither is a profit path.
```

Wanting “higher profit and lower loss” does not move that expectation. It is not a feature flag.

### 4.2 $2,000 is a blow-up ceiling vs approved size (HEAD α=1)

Lot law in this lab: **1.00 lot XAU = 100 oz** (`GoldOuncesPerLot = 100m`). `MaxPositionQuantity = 5` is **APPROVED** (strict `>`). Policy `AllocationFactor` is **`1m`** (`XauUsdOneToOneCopyPolicy` L66). `QuantityNormalizer` does `sourceLots * 1` then clamps to `GoldLots.MaxQuantity = 5`.

```text
1.00 lot XAU  ≈  100 oz     (lab convention; confirm on dest SecurityList before any send)
5.00 lots     =  500 oz
$1 / oz       =  $500   =  entire MaxLossPerTrader
$4 / oz       =  $2,000 =  entire MaxDailyExecutionLoss
$6 / oz       =  $3,000 =  entire MaxPortfolioDrawdown
```

| Ticket the engine will approve | Physical | $4 / oz routine gold swing | vs daily latch |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 × **5.00** lots (α=1, source 5.00) | 500 oz | **$2,000** | **equals** `MaxDailyExecutionLoss` |
| Net **10** lots (`MaxXauNet`) | 1,000 oz | **$4,000** | **2×** the daily latch |
| Login 303310 class **2.00** source × α=1 | 200 oz | **$800** | **40%** of the latch on one name |
| 70 SHADOW names × 1:1 on 0.10–2.00 source | **ruin** | news gap | P500_S055 |

XAUUSD routinely prints **$4** in a quiet hour and **$10–$30** in a session. One legal ticket can **spend the entire “kill switch”** before the predicate is allowed to fire. After it fires, close is rejected. That is a **ruin ceiling**, not a trading edge.

Synthesis Stage D (still no send today): dest daily **$200–$500** then persist `STOP_NEW_EXECUTION` only; dest working cap **0.05** lot; dest net **0.15–0.30**. Lab `$2,000` next to a 5-lot qty cap and 1:1 allocation is internally inconsistent.
