## 4. Why gold scalps die (arithmetic, not hope)

### 4.1 Hold time is computed and then thrown away

`BaselineScorer.ComputeFeatures` averages `(ClosedAt − OpenedAt)` into `AverageHoldSeconds`. `Score()` / `FromBaseline()` **never read it**. Grep of product `*.cs` for `AverageHoldSeconds` = **three writes, zero consumers** (plus a scratch `_tmp_` file). A 163-second gold book and a 4-hour swing book can both land `SHADOW` at quality ≥ 70.

- dest RT spread (seed sketch **$4 / 0.10 lot**; **$40 / 1.00 lot**; allowed up to **$200 / 1.00 lot**),
- dest-touch slip vs source VWAP (unbounded; `MaxSlippage` dead),
- **zero dest fill** if decision − open > 15 s.

A 163 s mean hold is **10.9 × 15 s**. After ingest delay + FIX probe latency (sibling measured **20 s** TLS+Logon on `CTraderFixSession`, already **> 15 s**), the legal window is closed **before a dest order can exist**. Sibling `P500_CODE_105`: sub-3-minute gold would not survive FIX latency + dest spread. This slot agrees from the **cost-engine** side.

### 4.2 Allocation is 1:1 — scalps pay full dest width

`XauUsdOneToOneCopyPolicy.AllocationFactor = 1m`. `CopyTradingService` aliases that constant. Prior “0.05 hop” reports are **stale**. Every 0.10-lot gold scalp that somehow passed age/spread would hit dest at **0.10 lots**, not 0.005. Tiny `α` was the only size-side cost brake. It is gone. Size is not an edge; 1:1 **amplifies** dest spread dollars.

### 4.3 Age reject is adverse-selection, not alpha

If a later sender honored `SIGNAL_STALE`:

- Fast **winners** that complete in 30–90 s are often **un-copied** (stale by decision, or already flat).
- Fast **losers** that stay open (grid, hope, average-down) remain copyable longer.
- You can reject the open and still copy the close (`IsIncreasing` only).

That is how a +$4,950 source scalp book becomes dest **zero or negative** even before spread. The reject **reduces some late-entry loss**. It does **not** harvest the source scalp PnL.

### 4.4 No dest tape ⇒ no measured dest EV

`PersistDemoShadowAsync` returns without fills if `DestinationQuotes` is empty. Live host never inserts a quote (`BrokerCatalogSeed` does not). Hosted FIX is `35=A` then dispose. Overview `DestinationRealPnl` / `XauGross` / `XauNet` are literals **0**. Claiming “scalps survive after costs” would be fabricating a tape.
