A22 (same law the 11-line file compresses) forbids dest PnL inside the three scores:

- **I6** — as-of `T` may use only information available at `T`. No trade #n+1, no future balance, no future drawdown.
- **I8** — the three scores are **source-behavior**. Shadow/live destination P&L is used only in the **state gate**, never inside the three formulas.
- **I9** — `early_quality_score` must **not** rank traders by raw net P&L. The product *target* is future copyable profitability; that is a **later label**, not the first-3 measurement.
- **§4 Forbidden inputs** — `NET` in account currency, destination P&L, future trades.
- **§7.3** — “Include shadow/live P&L” is rejected (would leak vs unshadowed books).
- **§7.2** — at `N=3`, spec cap is `100 − U(3) = 82`. Stub 95.50 **violates** that cap. Even the spec’s 82 is still **not** dest PnL.

Architecture §19 / A22 §1.1 item 5: first-3 features are **inputs**. Future dest/copy net (trades #4–#23, CVaR, DD) is the **unmeasured Phase-6 target**. A 95.5 is the input side only.
