## 2. Angle check

The assigned defect is a **dashboard UI that drops logins until they have a score row** (operator cannot see ingested-but-unscored `Mt5Accounts`).

That defect **cannot originate in `OverviewPage.tsx`**:

- The page never lists accounts, logins, or trader rows. There is no table, no `map` over traders, no per-login visibility gate.
- The headline census is `data.totalAccounts` under label `"MT5 accounts"`. The page does not recompute, clamp, or filter that number by score.
- Score-adjacent cards (`xauTraders`, `tradersWithThreeTrades`, `watch`, `shadow`, `liveCandidates`, `riskBlocked`) are **separate** MetricCards. They sit beside `totalAccounts`; they do not replace it.
- Fetch path: `useOverview()` → `GET /api/overview` (`apps/web/src/api/hooks.ts` lines 4–6). No query params, no `score` predicate.
- `if (!data) return null` blanks the **entire** overview when the DTO is missing. It does not omit a subset of accounts that lack scores.

Supporting (out of slot, for classification only): `EfDashboardQueries.GetOverviewAsync` sets the first DTO field with `var accounts = await _db.Mt5Accounts.CountAsync(ct);` — all persisted accounts — then separately `var scores = await _db.TraderScores.ToListAsync(ct);` for the XAU / state cards. Unscored logins still increment `totalAccounts`. The known “only scored traders” hide lives on `GetTradersAsync` / `TradersPage.tsx` (`foreach` over `TraderScores`, not `Mt5Accounts`) and is **not this file**.

Empty PASS is the measured result for **this file**, not a claim that `/traders` shows every ingested login.

## 4. No-loss implication

**None on this path.** `OverviewPage` cannot hide an account from ingest, scoring, shadow, or live copy because it never enumerates accounts and never calls an execution API. It only paints `/api/overview` aggregates and `/api/ingest/status` broker JSON. Copy risk is display-only: the page states `Live FIX NewOrderSingle is off — no capital at risk from this dashboard` and shows `data.realCopyEnabled` as ON/OFF. Missing score rows can shrink the XAU / Watch / Shadow / Live-candidate cards (those counts come from `TraderScores`), but `MT5 accounts` still reflects `Mt5Accounts.Count`. An operator cannot lose or mis-size capital because this page omitted an unscored login — the page has no order, kill-switch, or sizing control.
