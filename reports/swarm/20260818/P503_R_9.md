REMOVE = stop ADMIT. AllowNewOpens=false. Do not open more dest risk.
FLATTEN = dest-only CloseExposure of already-copied dest/shadow inventory.
           REMOVE must not veto CloseExposure. Residual dest risk after source

Never flatten the MT5 source book.

## 1. Law this slot reviews (not invented here)

Architecture §40 / §64 + A71:

- `STOP_NEW_EXECUTION` / roster REMOVE block **new** copy. Dest book is not left to die of old age.
- Flatten / source-driven close share dest class `CLOSE_EXPOSURE`. They do **not** share trigger or send conjunction.
- A71 **G19**: trader not LIVE — CLOSE of linked dest still **A**llowed.
- A71 **G20**: `PAUSED` / `RISK_BLOCKED` / `DISQUALIFIED` — OPEN **B**lock, CLOSE **A**llow. Pause stops **new** copy, not dest exits.
- A71 **G21–G23**: max loss / daily loss / DD engage **stop-new**. Do **not** trap a losing dest.
- A71 **G04**: source-driven live close still needs `REAL_COPY` in v1. Flatten CLOSE **may** send with the flag false (A48). Shadow never sends.

The reasons `CopyRosterEngine` ejects a name (`STATE_RISK_BLOCKED`, `SIZE_PATTERN`, `XAU_BOOK_TURNED_NEGATIVE`, `CONSECUTIVE_LOSSES_*`, `DRAWDOWN_FROM_PEAK`, `NO_LONGER_ELIGIBLE_*`) are exactly the snapshots that make `IsTraderEligible` false.
