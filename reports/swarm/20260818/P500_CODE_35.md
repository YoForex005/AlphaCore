1. **This process does not copy live.** Assigned file: `REAL_COPY_EXECUTION_ENABLED` **false (forced)**; live `35=D` NewOrderSingle **OFF — method does not exist**; intents stay **SHADOW-only**. There is no dest fill to “survive” into. `destinationRealPnl` is a constructor literal **0**.

3. **Even a hypothetical live hop fails the product’s own XAU guards.** Architecture §36: stale XAU copy **destroys expected edge**. Default `MaxSourceSignalAge = 15s` vs a ≤180 s hold. Default `MaxAllowedSpread = 2.0` USD and `MaxPriceMove = 3.0` USD are the same order as a gold scalp target. Two-way dest spread (buy ask / sell bid) is paid **twice** on a round trip that may last only tens of seconds. A 3-minute FIX gap is the **normative expire-don’t-copy** story (§63) — the same duration as the entire scalp.

Logged-on FIX is not a fill. Assigned file: QUOTE + TRADE TLS logon **true**; that is session, not source-to-fill latency, not dest PnL.
