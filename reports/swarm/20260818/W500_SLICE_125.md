## No-loss implication

Pump flags are `PUMP_MODE_GROUPS | PUMP_MODE_USERS | PUMP_MODE_POSITIONS` — data pump, not trade send.

If the MetaQuotes image cannot load, `ConnectCore` fails closed (`PlatformNotSupportedException` or `InvalidOperationException` on `Initialize`/`CreateManager`/`Connect`) and `_connected` stays false. `Ensure()` then refuses reads. A failed DLL load cannot reduce broker equity.
