§59 (L2218–2219) lists two **separate** authorized verbs: `activate stop-new-orders` vs `request emergency flatten`. Both must be audited.

§64 is **source-close handling** of an already-mapped dest remainder (`CLOSE_EXPOSURE` / `REDUCE_EXPOSURE`). It is **not** a book-wide market blast and it is **not** a Manager close of the source ticket.

### 2.2 Three close-like operations (D102 / A48 / A71) — none of them touch MT5 source

| Operation | Trigger | Book | Qty law | Auth |

- Close source MT5 or shadow.
- Guess quantity from source lots.
