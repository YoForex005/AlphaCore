## 3. No-loss implication

`BrokerCatalogSeed` cannot omit, page-cut, hardcode-four, or one-login-shrink the manager-visible trader set because it never fetches users. It cannot size, flatten, or copy destination risk from a partial login set. TRADE FIX seed already records **NewOrderSingle off**. Worst case inside this type is inserting two broker rows (one manager identity each), `XAUUSD`, kill-switch `None`, and disconnected FIX-state rows.

Residual completeness risk (`UserGetByGroup` / `UserLogins` miss, empty group, one-shot ingest, `ListLoginsAsync` empty until live sync) belongs to `NativeMt5BrokerConnector.ReadAccountsForGroup` / `DealIngestionService` / the ingest host, not this seeder.
