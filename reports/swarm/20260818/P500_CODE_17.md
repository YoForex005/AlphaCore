## Profit implication

Hard-false **blocks** live profit. It does not protect it.

- 8463 catalog accounts cannot become destination LIVE books through this pin.
- Achiever scores that land `SHADOW` (high quality, low risk) stay paper/`SHADOW_ONLY`. Winner tape is not copied as real cTrader exposure.
- Starwave `deals-done scored 0` means that book is not even in the scorer loop; zero chance of a live harvest from that cohort this pass.
- Dashboard dest real P&amp;L is **structurally 0**, independent of source PnL quality.
- `VolumeConverter` cannot create profit either: it only scales MT5 native volume → lots for reconstruction. Wrong scale would mis-size a future send; today there is no send.

If the question is “does hard-false keep us from giving back open profit?” — no live book exists to protect. Opportunity cost is the entire destination book.

---

## Lower-loss implication

Hard-false **does** lower loss (capital protection), together with other off-switches — not because `VolumeConverter` refuses LIVE.

- N=3 early evidence cannot auto-promote. Architecture / `docs/scoring.md` require SHADOW max. The pin is **stricter than required** (false for every state / every N).
- Application never calls `CanPromoteToLive`. Persist will copy whatever `SuggestedState` is. Safety today is “LIVE is not in the reachable set,” not “a gate evaluated LIVE then denied.”
- `REAL_COPY=false` + no NewOrderSingle builder: FIX may be LoggedOn and still cannot debit destination capital. Risk to capital from this process: **NONE** (`SAFE_BY_ABSENCE` of `35=D` plus flags).
- Demo SHADOW only: shadow PnL is Σ slippage, not real P&amp;L. A bad Achiever book cannot lose live money through this path.
- VolumeConverter residual: if promotion were later flipped true without dest-grid conversion, Manager scale `10_000` vs Extended `100_000_000` would mis-lot a send. That loss path is **not armed**.

Net: **protecting against live loss, blocking live profit, not protecting profit.** Assigned file is the wrong SUT for the angle.
