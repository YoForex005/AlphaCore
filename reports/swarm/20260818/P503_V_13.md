## 3. Where losers **are** left / returned (adversarial residuals)

These do **not** make remove/flatten “missing.” They **do** falsify a blanket “losers stay off the roster.”

### 3.1 Re-ADMIT after T2 / T3 REMOVE (latch hole)

Sequence on a still-green book that printed L-L-L or gave back ≥40% of peak:

1. Tick N: `onRoster=true` → `RemoveAndFlatten` → `REMOVED:CONSECUTIVE_LOSSES_3` or `REMOVED:DRAWDOWN_FROM_PEAK` + dest flatten intents.
2. Tick N+1 (≤20 s): `Status != ADMITTED` → `alreadyOnRoster=false` → streak/DD/net checks **skipped** → `IsTraderEligible` still true → `Action=Admit` → `row.Status = "ADMITTED"` (L142–143). Admit-on-existing-row does **not** reset `Action` back to `OpenExposure`.
3. Same hosted loop then `GenerateShadowIntentsAsync` copies new source opens.

T1 (`net<=0`) does **not** re-enter: eligibility `XAU_BOOK_NOT_PROFITABLE` yields `Keep` / `NOT_YET_*`, row stays `REMOVED`. That cut is sticky **by accident** of the policy, not a latch.

P503_S03 §4: *“A later winning XAU trade must not silently re-admit.”* HEAD will re-admit **without** a later winner if T2/T3 fired while Σ XAU stayed `> 0`.

### 3.2 First ADMIT ignores T2 / T3

A name that never had `ADMITTED` and already has streak ≥3 or `dd/peak ≥ 0.40` but `XauNetPnl > 0` is `AUTO_ADMIT`. Losers (by S03 T2/T3) are **put on** the roster, not left from a prior admit.

### 3.3 Open unrealized losers stay if the login stays eligible

`ShouldFlattenOpenCopy` (`unrealizedPnl <= -150`) has **0** product callers (`grep` `src/`: definition + tests only). `Decide` never sees dest MTM. A still-green admitted book can keep a −$151 (or −$15,000) open dest copy. That is a **position** leak, not a roster-status leak. `P503_AUTO_ROSTER.md` L20 “$150 cap is ready” is **false** as a hop claim.

### 3.4 No trader-state latch

REMOVE does not write `TraderScore.CurrentState = RISK_BLOCKED`. `TraderState.PAUSED` has **0** writers on this hop. A dashboard that keys off score state still shows SHADOW after `REMOVED:`.

### 3.5 Mild give-back stays ADMITTED (by design)

2 consecutive losses, or 39% of peak, or net still `+1` after a dump: `Keep`. Slot law is the numeric cuts, not “any loser.”

### 3.6 Paper flatten ≠ dest inventory gone

Even when REMOVE fires, dest lots are not closed at Pepperstone. `FLATTEN_LOSS_CUT` expires in 15 s. If a sender is later armed without a dest-id flatten run, residual dest is **orphaned**. Today that residual is **$0** because nothing was sent.
