## 1. Why this slot is lower-loss, not higher-profit

Architecture §3 target is **future destination-net PnL inside risk limits**. Two different questions:

| Question | Mechanism | Moves dest EV? |
|---|---|---|
| **Higher profit** | Copy a tiny, filtered subset with +EV **after** dest costs | Yes, if and only if that subset exists and is proven on a real quote tape |
| **Lower loss** (this slot) | At most one dest order per intent; never a second `35=D` while venue outcome is unknown | Caps **duplicate** loss. Does **not** flip the sign of a −EV book |

Blind retry after “I sent, then TCP died” is how a 0.05-lot hedge becomes **0.10** (or a 5-lot cap becomes **10**) on **one** retail Pepperstone login. Official cTrader Logon is `141=Y` (seq reset). FIX resend **cannot** recover the original `35=D`. Persist-before-send + `OrderStatusRequest` is the only recovery. That is **loss containment**.

It is not a filter. It does not see `RISK_BLOCKED`. It does not see demo `yo-2step`. It does not haircut spread. Copying all **8463** logins with perfect ClOrdID hygiene still imports the **−$241,580** martingale tail **once**. Once is enough to ruin the dest (`P500_BOOK_11`, `P500_S055`).

Wanting both “no duplicate fills” and “the book is profitable” does not make the second true.

## 5. Duplicate-size math (why “retry unknown” is dest death)

One retail Pepperstone login (`P500_S055`). Working dest cap the synthesis asks for is **0.05** lot, not `MaxPositionQuantity=5`.

| Failure | Dest gold after one intent |
|---|---|
| Persist 11 → send → ER fill | **1×** requested qty (correct) |
| Send → crash → new 11 → send again (first landed) | **2×** qty. 0.05 → 0.10. 5-lot cap → **10 lots** = `MaxXauNet` in **one** duplicated ticket |
| Same 11 retried after `141=Y` and first landed | Venue may reject, or you hold a working order you already thought dead and then send a **replacement** 11 anyway |
| Unknown + `OrderStatusRequest` adopts fill | **1×**. Lower-loss. |
| Copy 29 `RISK_BLOCKED` with perfect 11 hygiene | **1× the −$241k shape**. Account death without needing a duplicate |
| Copy all **8463** with perfect 11 hygiene | Same tail + noise, **once each**. Ruin. Persist does not save you |

`MaxXauNetExposure=10` / `MaxPositionQuantity=5` / `MaxMarginUsage=0.70` are blow-up caps (`P500_S055`). A duplicated 5-lot gold ticket **is** the net cap. Persist-before-send is how you avoid walking into that cap by accident. It is not how you make money.
