- **This file is not a profit engine.** It describes how a hedge venue receives orders. LoggedOn + instrument map + 30-point slippage flag do not produce expectancy. Measured dest PnL is **0** because nothing is sent and the overview field is a literal zero.
- **“Copy winning trades” (L5) is adverse selection on recovery books.** High-win-rate gold grids look like Achiever winners until the first trend day. Ranking / copying those prints **selects** the left tail, not edge.
- **Filters that do exist cannot be used as a profit screen.** Averaging +20 risk cannot keep a green book out of `SHADOW` (`risk < 40`). Mild martingale +35 also cannot. Early quality **rewards** recovered NET. That is the opposite of harvesting durable dest edge.

- **Shadow / demo P&L is not dest edge.** Peak-lot VWAP simulation (adjacent persist) does not replay add-down path or open-pyramid MAE. Do not treat SHADOW quality as a copyable edge after FIX costs.
- **30-point slippage** is a fill-quality flag, not alpha. On XAU it is a few dollars per 0.01 lot, not a ruin bound.
