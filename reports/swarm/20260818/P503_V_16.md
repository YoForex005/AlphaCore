### 6.2 `Keep` / `NOT_YET_*` is not a seat

Off-roster not-yet-eligible returns `Action = Keep` (L86–92). Naming is sloppy. Status is never written `ADMITTED`. Hopper `continue`s. Not on the roster.

### 6.3 Orphan `ADMITTED` without a `TraderScore`

`TickRosterAsync` walks scores only. A score-delete leaving an `ADMITTED` row would stay until something else overwrites it. Hopper also walks scores, so it would not copy. `ExecuteDemoCopyAsync` **does** walk `ADMITTED` seats without requiring a score (L542–546). Theoretical orphan-send. No product deleter of scores found this slot. Residual.

### 6.4 Policy alone does not remove

`XauUsdOneToOneCopyPolicy` rejects (`XAU_BOOK_NOT_PROFITABLE`) and **never** mutates roster. If someone called only `Evaluate`, a previously admitted loser would get no new opens **and** no REMOVE. HEAD hop **does** call `Decide` first. P503_R_20 “policy has no auto-remove” is true of the policy file and **stale** as a hop claim.
